Ádám János Csillag

Language engineer

I'm a language engineer with more than twenty years in the translation industry. From 2004 to 2026 I worked at espell, a Hungarian language service provider, and since September 2026 I've been self-employed.

I'm a computer engineer by training (MSc, Budapest University of Technology and Economics, 2004), and I work in Hungarian and English, which I've spoken since childhood.

I also build software for translators: tools that respect the formats translators are handed, never lose their work, and keep out of the way while they translate.

Projects
